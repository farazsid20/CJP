Accountability Lens

Corruption Awareness

Corruption ko sirf scandal ke roop mein nahi, daily youth impact ke roop mein samajhna zaroori hai. Yahan issue areas aur possible system fixes dono diye gaye hain.

Core Approach

Expose + Explain + Repair

  • Opaque process ko plain language mein explain karna
  • Impact ko youth life se connect karna
  • Practical, trackable reforms propose karna
Education Sector

Campus-Level Irregularities

Examples

  • Admission irregularities and inconsistent seat allocation
  • Resource misuse in facilities and student funds

Possible Solutions

  • Transparent digital systems for admissions and fee visibility
  • Digital monitoring of resource allocation and complaints
Employment

Recruitment Related Corruption

Examples

  • Recruitment irregularities in selection processes
  • Favoritism over merit and transparent evaluation

Possible Solutions

  • Merit transparency through published selection rubrics
  • Public accountability through audit-friendly job dashboards
Local Governance

Slow Systems, Hidden Decisions

Examples

  • Administrative delays with no public tracking
  • Lack of transparency around status and approvals

Possible Solutions

  • Public dashboards for pending work and service timelines
  • Citizen reporting systems that create visible accountability
