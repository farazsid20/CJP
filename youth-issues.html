Issue Map

Youth Issues

Education, employment, mental health, aur digital safety ke key pressure points ko yahan problem-solution format mein break kiya gaya hai.

Why It Matters

Pressure sirf personal nahi hota

Jab systems weak hote hain, youth struggle private lagti hai. CJP us struggle ko public language aur practical next steps deta hai.

Focus: awareness ke saath actionable civic framing.
Education

Learning Ke Naam Par Pressure

Problems

  • High fees and unclear cost structure
  • Skill gap between curriculum and industry
  • Employment pressure from an early stage

Possible Solutions

  • Skill-based education modules alongside academics
  • Internship ecosystem with local employers
  • Career guidance programs from school to college
Employment

Job Market Ka Broken Entry Gate

Problems

  • Unemployment and unstable hiring cycles
  • Low salaries despite higher qualifications
  • Experience barrier for freshers

Possible Solutions

  • Startup support for youth-led local ventures
  • Short-cycle skill training with measurable outcomes
  • Industry partnerships with campus hiring bridges
Mental Health

Invisible Stress, Real Damage

Problems

  • Stress linked to academics, money, and family expectations
  • Social pressure and comparison culture
  • Career anxiety during transitions

Possible Solutions

  • Counseling awareness through schools and colleges
  • Peer support communities with safe moderation
  • Public messaging that normalizes asking for help
Digital Safety

Online Risk Is A Youth Issue

Problems

  • Cyber scams targeting students and job-seekers
  • Privacy risks from poor digital literacy

Possible Solutions

  • Cyber awareness programs in campuses and local groups
  • Data protection education in simple language
